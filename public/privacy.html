Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Draft. This policy describes how the current version of Crown works. It will be reviewed and updated before Crown is released on the App Store.

The short version

  • Crown does not require an account.
  • Your collection, photos, serial numbers and prices stay on your device. Only the photo you scan is sent out, to identify the watch.
  • Crown has no ads, no advertising trackers and does not sell your data.

What Crown stores on your device

To work, Crown keeps the following on your phone: the watches in your collection, the photos you take or choose, serial numbers, purchase prices, condition and box-and-papers details, service dates, your wishlist, your answers to the setup questions, and your settings (theme, language, currency). This data is not sent to us.

Camera and photos

Crown asks for camera access so you can photograph a watch dial and identify the model. You can also pick a photo from your library. Photos are reduced in size and saved with your collection on your device.

To identify a watch, Crown sends the photo you scan to Google Gemini, a third-party AI service provided by Google, through Crown's own server. The photo is used only to name the brand, model and reference. Crown's server does not store photos. Crown currently uses the free version of the Gemini API, under which Google may use submitted content to improve its products; please avoid photos that show people or personal details. Before Crown is released on the App Store, recognition will move to the paid version, and this policy will be updated.

Backups you create

If you export a backup, Crown creates a file on your device that contains your collection, including photos. Where that file goes (for example iCloud Drive or another app) is your choice and is governed by that service's terms.

Purchases

Crown Pro subscriptions are handled by Apple. We do not receive your payment card details.

Children

Crown is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal data.

Deleting your data

Remove a watch to delete its details and photo, or use Settings › Reset to delete everything. Deleting the app also removes its data from your device.

Changes

If Crown starts collecting or sharing data in a new way (for example accounts, cloud sync or live recognition), this policy will be updated first and the date above will change.
